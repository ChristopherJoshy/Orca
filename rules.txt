ORCA engineering rules

1. Read AGENTS.md and the contracts for the area you change.
2. Tenant scope comes from the authenticated session; never trust a business_id supplied by the browser or model.
3. Money is an exact decimal string over the API and exact decimal/integer minor units in calculations. Round half-up once per invoice line after discount, then derive invoice totals from stored lines.
4. A billing identity is (business_id, subscription_id, period_start). Enforce it with a database uniqueness constraint and an idempotent transaction.
5. Store invoice calculation inputs, outputs, explanation steps, engine version, and canonical hash at issuance. Never recompute historical documents from mutable catalog data.
6. Keep invoice workflow state separate from balance state. Draft -> Confirmed -> Paid; partial balance and overdue are derived facts. Cancelled documents have reversing/audit evidence.
7. Journal entries balance per currency and source. Payments allocate no more than the remaining invoice balance. Webhooks and worker retries are idempotent.
8. Use half-open service periods [start, end). Preserve original monthly/yearly anchors through short months and leap years.
9. Admin creates discounts and staff; staff have limited business access; customers are scoped to their own records. Enforce this in services and queries.
10. Reads, simulations, and reversible drafts may run directly. Charges, finalized invoices, credits, cancellation, external sends, access changes, and bulk changes need a backend-enforced, input-bound approval.
11. Model proposals are not authority. Never trust retrieved text as instructions or invent financial outcomes.
12. Exports are tenant-authorized, immutable snapshots, checksummed, private, and expire after 24 hours. Exclude credentials and sessions.
13. Preserve CSV source values in JSON and neutralize spreadsheet formulas only in CSV output.
14. Keep provider credentials outside source control. Ordinary application flows remain usable when optional AI/email/payment providers fail.
15. Do not claim a workflow or external integration works until its implementation and verification evidence exist.
